A dense news poster that sums up the whole of Friday 13 November 2026 in
a single picture — 28 verified stories, not six. A full-width lead band
opens on the Ukraine peace talks (five nations chosen to speak for
Europe), followed by a uniform 3×3 grid of nine numbered picture-led
stories: the first Trump–Xi–Putin three-way meeting, the FAA grounding
every US departure, Nvidia falling under $4 trillion, Iran's radar claim
in Bahrain, Starship Flight 15 reaching orbit, the strike on Gaza's
Nasser Hospital, Pope Leo XIV leading Mass in Chiclayo, a Kyiv hospital
set ablaze by a Russian drone, and the bus-truck crash in Assam. Below
them, an "ALSO ON FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER" list adds 18 more stories from the
same day — the New Orleans riverboat, the Microsoft outage, the
Turkey/Qatar defence pact, the Hormuz gas tanker, Yemen's displaced, the
Moscow car bomb, Takaichi's first China visit, Pakistan's border raids,
North Korea's reshaped flag, the Nairobi collapse, Nigeria clearing its
Ebola contacts, the RAF Lakenheath drone scare, US wholesale inflation,
Boebert losing her seat, Shuttle Endeavour standing upright in Los
Angeles, U2's new album, Pochettino leaving for Manchester United, and
Japan beating South Korea 9-5.

Every story was verified as reported on Friday 13 November 2026, and every
headline is written fresh from the verified facts. v4 keeps the dense
"many stories in one picture" structure of v3 but is a clearly different
poster: a navy banner masthead with white and amber sans type instead of
a giant serif date, ink-sketch illustrations with pale washes instead of
flat full-colour vector scenes, navy square number badges, Liberation Sans
throughout, a lead band plus a 3×3 grid instead of a 3+4+4 grid, and a
two-column also-on list.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` (JPEG, 1024x1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
No network, no dependencies beyond the Python standard library.
